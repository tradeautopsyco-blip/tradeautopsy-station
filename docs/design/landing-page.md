# Landing Page Design Notes

Rules for `tradeautopsy.in` marketing pages. Built on [principles.md](./principles.md).

## The job of the page

Four answers in order: What is this? Is it for me? Does it work? Is it credible? You get **5–10 seconds**.

## Hero

- One headline, one subheadline, one CTA.
- Headline = what + who + why; subhead literal if headline is clever.
- Say what it does, not just the benefit.
- Qualify at the top (live brokers/platform from CLAIM-REGISTRY).
- No equal-weight two-column hero; don't fill the whole screen; no looping motion behind copy.
- Differentiate: intervene *before* execution.

## Copy, product visuals, CTAs, proof, structure, visual rules

Follow the full checklist in the product repo issue docs and [principles.md](./principles.md) §7–9. Pre-ship: 5-second test, headline skim, one teal CTA repeated, real product visual, no banned AI tells, claims backed, mobile + Lighthouse.
