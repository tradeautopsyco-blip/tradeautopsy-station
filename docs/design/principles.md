# TradeAutopsy Design Principles

Distilled from YC Design Review talks (Stripe, Linear, Cursor, and others). Applies to every surface: landing page, Station, Notch, Console, emails.

Companion notes: [landing-page.md](./landing-page.md) · [product-design.md](./product-design.md)

## 1. Clarity before cleverness

A visitor or user must answer **"What is this?"** then **"Is it for me?"** before anything else matters.

**For us:** say what the product does today, not internal vocabulary (signal stream, L3, dual-layer scoring).

## 2. Use the user's words, not ours

Put traders' exact phrases in headlines. Internal terms never appear in user-facing copy.

## 3. Pick one lane, for one ideal customer

The site names only what is live today. The live list is `issues/compliance/CLAIM-REGISTRY.md`.

## 4. Craft is a trust signal — especially where money is involved

We sit between a trader and their broker. A typo in a kill-switch dialog is a trust failure.

## 5. Quality order: utility → usability → craft

Ship minimum viable *quality*; hold what hurts first impression.

## 6. Fight the pull to mediocrity

Use saved time to push signature moments past 7/10, not to ship more 7s.

## 7. Don't accept AI defaults — you are the editor

Start from our brand tokens and voice. Ban: heavy bold everywhere, purple gradients, glows, uppercase kickers, decorative KPI tiles, cursor glows, fade-in everywhere, fake 10x metrics.

## 8. Motion must communicate

Animate only to draw attention to the next thing that matters.

## 9. Show, don't tell

Real product > illustration. Our output is the intervention moment.

## 10. Consistency is one component per behaviour

Brand, site, product, emails, overlay — one story.

## 11. Walk the store

Walk essential journeys end to end; friction log; red/yellow/green.

## 12. Design includes speed and how it's built

Latency and failure modes are design decisions.

## 13. Keep the human in the loop and in control

TAI emits signals; it never trades — visible in UI.

## 14. The codebase is the design source of truth

Tokens and components live in code. Prototype with real data.

## 15. Talk to a trader every week

Watch users during market hours under stress.

## Brand constraints (from AGENTS.md)

- Dark theme, one teal accent for primary action and key state only.
- JetBrains Mono for numbers, Inter for UI.
- GlassCard restrained — no glow stacks.
- Adjectives: **calm, direct, protective, precise**.
