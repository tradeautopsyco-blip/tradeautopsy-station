## Law this chrome must keep

- DualNoBlend: two live books stay two names, never one blended last.
- Vendor is never last. The bar may say **Eligible** / **Not eligible**. It must not say `licensed_history · unsupported — licensed_histo…`.
- Founder does **not** name an India history vendor in chrome. The user adds a key in Backend Box. If the binding is **eligible** (declared Identity + host fence + key not a URL, or public AMFI), the obtain query may run. If not eligible, the hole stays a hole.
- Quote / Instruments / Account stay independent. Quote dark does not paint vendor last.

## Why the screenshot is ugly

The production top bar concatenates: title, 0.00, Waiting, Binance live capsule, full vendor fence string, Quote unavailable, Instruments, Account, Declaration. Apple materials want **one accessory, color only for exceptions, detail in a popover from the trigger** — not eight capsules fighting.

**Verdict:** **A — Books accessory.** Production Notch top bar now uses one glass cluster of live books and a Status control. Holes (Quote / Instruments / Account / Eligible query) live in the popover. Vendor slugs and `fenceLine` stay out of the bar. B and C are not shipping.
